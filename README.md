## About me

I'm a second-year **BSc Computer Science student at the University of Liverpool**, particularly interested in **artificial intelligence, machine learning, algorithms, and the mathematics behind intelligent systems**. I finished my first year with a **First-Class result** and now serve as a **demonstrator for COMP109: Foundations of Computer Science**, supporting other students with foundational CS concepts.

I enjoy going beyond using a model or library to understand **why an algorithm works**, from linear algebra and calculus to optimisation and backpropagation. I combine mathematical study with hands-on Python experiments and small software projects.

- **Current focus:** Neural networks, optimisation, mathematical foundations of ML, and practical AI projects.
- **Teaching:** COMP109 Foundations of Computer Science demonstrator at the University of Liverpool.
- **Community:** Founded and led a senior mathematics club and have helped students through online maths learning communities.
- **Collaboration:** Interested in AI/ML, algorithms, mathematical modelling, and thoughtful open-source projects.
