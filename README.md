# 👤 About Me

🧠 I'm interested in artificial intelligence, machine learning and the mathematical foundations of computing. I particularly enjoy understanding the theory behind the technologies I work with, rather than simply learning how to use them.

🔭 **Currently working on:**  
A mathematical study of forward propagation and backpropagation in artificial neural networks, combining mathematical derivations, numerical examples and Python experiments.

🌱 **Currently exploring:**  
Linear algebra, differential calculus, optimisation, neural networks, machine learning and computational methods.

🤝 **Looking to collaborate on:**  
Projects involving AI, machine learning, algorithms, mathematical modelling and problem-solving.

💬 **Ask me about:**  
Mathematics, Python, artificial intelligence, neural networks or effective ways of learning complex topics.

⚡ **A little more about me:**  
I enjoy independent learning, exploring ideas in depth and connecting different areas of mathematics and computer science. I also run a small Telegram channel called Neptune, where I share ideas, music, poetry and other things I find interesting.
